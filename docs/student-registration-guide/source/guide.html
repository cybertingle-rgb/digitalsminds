Welcome
Welcome to Digitals Minds

Live online training, in batches, with a real team behind you

Digitals Minds runs practical training programs, such as e-commerce and marketplace training, that are taught live online by our team. You join a batch, a group of students who start together and follow the same class schedule.

The website is where you apply and pay. Your student portal is where you find your batch details, class schedule, joining links, onboarding steps, documents, notices and support.

Good to know: classes happen live (for example on Zoom or Google Meet). The portal doesn't contain recorded lessons, videos or quizzes. It organises everything around your live classes.

In this guide

Every step a new student takes, in order, with screenshots of the real website and portal.

1 · Choose and apply

Explore programs, read the fee and batch details, and send your application. No account needed yet.

2 · Pay and send your details

Pay the fee yourself using the accounts on your private application page, then send us your transaction ID and receipt.

3 · Verification and activation

Our team checks the money has arrived. Only then is your seat confirmed and your student account created.

4 · Your student portal

Set your password, log in, complete onboarding and join your live classes.

If something's wrong
If a payment can't be verified

Fix the details and send them again

If our team can't find your payment (for example, a wrong transaction ID), they mark it not verified with a reason.

  1. 1Your application goes back to Payment pending and shows the reason: "We couldn't verify your last payment: …".
  2. 2Check your payment, then send the correct details again in Your payment details.
We also email you "We couldn't verify your payment" with a new link to your application. Earlier links stop working, so use the newest email.

FAQ
Frequently asked questions

Answers based on how the platform works

Do I need an account to apply?

No. You apply as a visitor. Your student account is created only after your payment is verified.

How long does verification take?

Our team checks each payment by hand against the account statement. You'll get an email as soon as it's verified; there's no automatic approval.

Can I pay in instalments?

The fee is paid in full, in PKR, before your enrollment is confirmed. Fees are non-refundable once paid (see the Refund Policy).

Which payment methods can I use?

The ones listed under How to pay on your application page: bank transfer, JazzCash or EasyPaisa, when offered.

I didn't receive an email.

Check your spam folder. Then use Find your application with your email and reference to get a new link.

My payment wasn't verified.

The reason is shown on your application page and emailed to you with a fresh link. Check the details and send them again.

The batch I want is full.

A full batch stops taking applications. Choose another open batch, or contact us about the next one.

When can I join a class?

From your Schedule page: Join class switches on 15 minutes before the start. Reminders are emailed 1 day and 1 hour before.

Are lessons available as videos?

No. Programs are taught live online; the portal doesn't host recorded lessons.

Who can see my CNIC and CV?

Only our enrollment staff. They are never shown publicly.
